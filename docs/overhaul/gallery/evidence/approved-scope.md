# Approved overhaul scope

The user selected cinematic anime direction A and asked to implement original recommendations 1–8. The original audit table is preserved below. The voyage loop was also implemented to satisfy the requested replayability; it is additional to those eight packages. The comparison gallery groups nine sections accordingly. Acceptance criteria remain requirements, not completion claims.

| Order | Work package | Concrete implementation | Acceptance criterion | Effort / dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reliable entry and control | Fix launch layout, Escape state, focus/input clearing, chapter loadout preservation; separate safe onboarding from the current immediate fleet fight. | New player selects any ship and reaches controlled sailing by pointer or keyboard on agreed desktop sizes. Pause freezes simulation and resumes predictably. | M; first |
| 2 | One hero ship standard | Rebuild Sunny with a full sculpted hull, correct waterline, authored deck hierarchy, sail tension, rigging, cannon anchors and three damage states. Produce near/mid/far LODs. | Looks complete from chase, broadside and deck cameras; silhouette survives a thumbnail; no visible hull gaps, waterline artifacts or major LOD popping. | L; chosen direction |
| 3 | Ocean and atmospheric unity | Keep the authoritative long-wave field. Add visual small-scale normals, wind-aligned foam, hull contact foam, connected curved wakes, bow spray and shore foam. Drive sky, sun, fog, water and effects from one weather profile. | Turning leaves a curved wake; stopping dissipates it; storms visibly change the entire scene; night ships read through moonlight/lantern accents. CPU/GPU water-contact tests continue to pass. | L; rendering baseline |
| 4 | Broadside aiming and feedback | Add hold-to-aim port/starboard camera framing and visible firing arc/range cues. Support lead adjustment and clear aim assist settings. Stagger guns, recoil mounts, animate crew reloads, impact wood/water differently. | In a duel, a player can explain a miss, identify the ready side without reading a dense panel, and distinguish chain/heavy/explosive effects. Camera keeps the selected target in view. | L; 1–3 |
| 5 | Damage and finishing | Drive hull breaches, torn sail variants, broken rails, fire, flooded list and sinking from authoritative damage thresholds/events. Offer a deliberate surrender/loot decision. | Damage remains visible after the flash; all finish states award once; a disabled enemy has a clear resolution rather than an inert hull and a text notification. | L; 4 |
| 6 | Crew as a tactical resource | Allocate a bounded crew pool among helm, guns, repair and special preparation. Use readable station animations and short context cues. Reuse skeletons for background crew, reserve bespoke animation for captain beats. | Assigning repair visibly slows gun throughput or helm effectiveness; crew move/react coherently; the tradeoff changes a battle outcome. | L; 4–5 |
| 7 | Ship-specific specials | Preserve current mechanical effects but give them readable wind-up, active and recovery phases. Sunny burst = charge/nozzle blast/wake gap; Polar Tang = actual dive state and resurfacing window; Moby Dick = telegraphed traveling pressure wave. | The three reference specials differ in both counterplay and silhouette, and enemies telegraph them before damage. Effects never invent gameplay hits. | L; 3–6 |
| 8 | Meaningful archipelago | Author one harbor, a sea arch, a reef ambush lane and one fort encounter. Use procedural assembly for connective sea. Add authoritative landmark IDs, shore collision and discoveries. | Every prominent nearby landmark offers a service, route choice, hazard, encounter or reward. Visual land and collision agree. | L; world-state refactor |
