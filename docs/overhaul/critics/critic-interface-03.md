# Blind interface and gameflow acceptance review 03

**Verdict: partial acceptance.** The launch, pause, crew choices, and demonstrated salvage-to-refit loop are understandable and functionally supported. I would hold final mobile combat interface acceptance for the target-visibility issue below. The screenshots show a usable game structure, but passing input checks does not by itself establish usable aiming or a complete voyage.

Review performed September 6, 2026 using only the supplied screenshots and JSON receipts in `ui-08`, `aim-08`, and `progression-07`. No source code, previous review reports, or browser were opened. This is an artifact review, not a live playtest or performance measurement.

## Concrete defects

1. **P1 — The landscape starboard aim camera can leave the selected target unclear or invisible.** In [landscape-starboard-touch-aim.png](../../../output/overhaul-gauntlet/aim-08/landscape-starboard-touch-aim.png), nearby red sail geometry fills approximately the left 40% of the display while the target card identifies a Marine Galleon at 173 m. No clear target vessel or usable target marker can be distinguished in the remaining view. The large central aim panel further consumes the sightline. [landscape-starboard-multitouch-reload.png](../../../output/overhaul-gauntlet/aim-08/landscape-starboard-multitouch-reload.png) has a close galleon occupying the left side, but the selected 159 m target is still not visually unambiguous. [desktop-starboard-aim.png](../../../output/overhaul-gauntlet/aim-08/desktop-starboard-aim.png) also shows a large foreground sail occupying much of the left side; the desktop has more room to recover the scene. The receipt confirms target IDs agree and the battery fires; that does not resolve the visibility problem. Keep aim camera framing clear of nearby geometry, and provide a strong visible target marker or an explicit edge indicator when the selected target is occluded or outside the view. Acceptance should include a short moving sequence in both batteries where a player can locate the selected ship and judge bearing while firing.

2. **P2 — Compact helm layouts remove movement feedback needed by the salvage objective.** [portrait-helm.png](../../../output/overhaul-gauntlet/ui-08/portrait-helm.png) and [landscape-helm.png](../../../output/overhaul-gauntlet/ui-08/landscape-helm.png) offer sail and steering buttons but show no readable speed or sail/throttle setting. The desktop equivalent displays `05 kn` plus a vertical setting bar. The task explicitly requires holding within 65 m for 12 seconds, so a touch player needs feedback that lowering sail has taken effect and the ship is slowing. Retain a compact speed and sail-setting display in both phone orientations. Landscape also reduces ship condition to hull alone, weakening feedback for repair/crew decisions; this can be made compact without hiding it entirely.

3. **P2 — Landscape vessel selection hides the comparison that makes the selection meaningful.** [landscape-embark.png](../../../output/overhaul-gauntlet/ui-08/landscape-embark.png) exposes all nine names and the primary launch CTA, but omits the selected vessel's role, speed/power/turn/hull comparison, and special ability. These are visible in [desktop-embark.png](../../../output/overhaul-gauntlet/ui-08/desktop-embark.png) and [portrait-embark.png](../../../output/overhaul-gauntlet/ui-08/portrait-embark.png). A new player can choose a name in landscape but cannot make the same informed ship choice. Add a compact selected-ship summary, or allow that information to be scrolled into view instead of removing it.

4. **P2 — Extraction success has contradictory leg labeling and ambiguous upgrade persistence copy.** [run-1-extracted.png](../../../output/overhaul-gauntlet/progression-07/run-1-extracted.png) and [run-2-extracted.png](../../../output/overhaul-gauntlet/progression-07/run-2-extracted.png) say `Welcome home`, show 175 banked, and correctly show zero completed contracts, but also label the closed voyage `LEG 2 / 3` after the demonstrated leg-one salvage. The preceding [reward screen](../../../output/overhaul-gauntlet/progression-07/run-1-earned-rewards.png) clearly says the chosen upgrade lasts for the rest of this voyage; the extraction screen then says `Your reward and fitted upgrades are saved.` This can sound as though the temporary upgrade is permanent. Show `Extracted after leg 1 of 3` or equivalent, and explicitly distinguish banked coins/permanent harbor refits from voyage upgrades that end with extraction.

## Evidence limits

- `aim-08` records ordinary launched simulation and trusted keyboard/mouse/CDP touch inputs, with no capture mode, stepping, state edits, or private scene access. Its 54 passing checks establish the recorded functional behavior, not aiming comfort, sustained FPS, physical-device touch ergonomics, or visual concept parity.
- `progression-07` uses real UI decisions and normal helm actions with accelerated deterministic simulation stepping. It proves the recorded earned-salvage/extraction/purchase/restore sequence. It is not a normal-time play session or a performance sample.
- Progression ends with `totalVoyages: 2`, `completedVoyages: 0`, and `rangefinder: 1`. No complete three-leg contract, final encounter, completion bonus, defeat loss, or full voyage branching acceptance can be inferred.
- The receipts are from different bundles: UI/aim use `index-BiV0tM2y.js` on port 4189; progression uses `index-BlvTWNaC.js` on port 4188. Treat the progression receipt as evidence for that captured build unless the same sequence is rechecked against the final candidate.
- No portrait aiming capture was supplied. Portrait menu/helm acceptance cannot be extended to portrait combat aiming.
